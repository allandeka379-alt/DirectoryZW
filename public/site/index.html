Questions

How do customers find me?

They message our WhatsApp number with what they need and where. We answer with the businesses that sell it nearby, verified businesses first, with their prices. One tap opens a chat with you.

Do I need a computer?

No. The website and dashboard work on any phone. You can also manage your listing on WhatsApp: type my business to our number.

How do you check businesses?

A field agent may visit to verify your business. Verified businesses are marked as verified and shown first, so customers can tell them apart.

Can I upload my whole price list?

Yes. Download our template, or use your own spreadsheet: save it as CSV, or copy the cells and paste them in. We show you what will be added or updated before anything changes.

How do I sign in?

Send dashboard to our WhatsApp number from the phone you registered with, and we reply with a sign-in link. No password to remember.
